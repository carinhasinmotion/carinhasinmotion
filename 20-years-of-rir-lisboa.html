20 Years of RIR Lisboa

Rock in Rio
Motion Designer
2024

A commemorative show celebrating 20 years of Rock in Rio Lisboa, using large-scale video projection on the main stage to revisit key moments from the festival’s two decades of history.

Project Overview

Created for Rock in Rio Lisboa’s 20th anniversary, the show looked back at the festival’s history through projected visual content across the main stage. As part of the motion design team, I developed visual layouts and animated sequences for different moments of the show, contributing to a large-scale production designed around the stage and its projection surface.

Key Highlights

  • Motion design for Rock in Rio Lisboa’s 20th anniversary show
  • Developed layouts and animated sequences for the main stage
  • Content created for large-scale video projection
  • Part of a show revisiting two decades of the festival’s history
